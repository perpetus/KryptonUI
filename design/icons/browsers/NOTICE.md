# Browser and service identification artwork

This directory contains browser identification artwork copied from
[Browser Logos](https://github.com/alrra/browser-logos), including channel-specific
logos. Related search-provider artwork is retained in
`design/icons/search_engines/`. The source revision of the original Browser
Logos import was not recorded.

The logos and product names remain the property of their respective owners.
They identify supported import sources, browser channels or search providers;
they are not Krypton-owned artwork and are not covered by the repository's
GPL-3.0 grant. Consult the original product owners' brand guidelines before
using these marks for another purpose. Their inclusion does not imply
endorsement or affiliation.

Additional onboarding artwork has separately retained MIT/CC0 notices and
source metadata under `design/icons/onboarding/licenses/`. Dia's official favicon
source is recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
